Privacy Policy

Expense & Travel Log · Effective September 29, 2026

The short version

The app does not collect, store or share any of your information. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores

Your reports, expenses, receipt photos and settings (such as your name, client and the email address you send reports to) are saved only on your device. The developer has no server and cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Camera and photos

The app uses the camera and your photo library only when you choose to scan or pick a receipt. Receipt text is read on the device; photos are not uploaded anywhere.

Sending reports

When you send a report, the app hands the spreadsheet and receipt photos to the app you choose (for example Mail or Gmail). What happens next is covered by that app's privacy policy. The app itself sends nothing.

Analytics, ads and tracking

None. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not share data with third parties.

Children

The app is intended for adults tracking work expenses and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Questions: ebrewmaster@gmail.com.
