Support

Questions or problems? Email ebrewmaster@gmail.com and include your device model and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

Where is my data stored? Only on your device. Reports and receipt photos never leave it unless you send a report yourself.

How do I send a report? Open the report and tap the paper-plane button. Choose Mail, or "Gmail or Other App…" to use the share sheet.

Can I change the mileage rate or report logo? Yes, in Settings (the gear icon).

What does "Scan Receipt" do? It uses the device camera to find and flatten the receipt, then reads the business name, date and total on the device to fill in the expense. Always check the details before saving.
